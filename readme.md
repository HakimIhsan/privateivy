ELITE IVY LEAGUE MENTORSHIP – CLIENT FAQ (UPDATED)
1. Will you write my essay or CV for me?
Answer:
We do not write essays or CVs on behalf of applicants. Every word originates from you.
Our role is to translate your experiences, achievements, and insights into a clear, structured, and compelling narrative.
Through in-depth interviews and feedback, we refine structure, flow, and language to Ivy League standards — always preserving your authentic voice.

2. Do you guarantee admission?
Answer:
No service can guarantee admission — it is solely at the discretion of the universities.
What we guarantee is full-cycle, founder-led mentorship, positioning your application in the strongest possible way, and supporting every step — from strategy to submission, including interview preparation and post-admission guidance such as visa support and financial aid.

3. Why is your Executive Advisory more expensive than Signature?
Answer:
Executive Advisory is white-glove, high-stakes, and all-inclusive:
Weekly 1-on-1 mentorship
Multi-school Ivy League strategy (up to 5 programs)
Crisis, waitlist, and reapplication management
Full family consultation and decision alignment
Dedicated visa guidance and mock interview preparation
Financial aid and scholarship application support
Full enrollment, retention, and graduation advisory
It’s not just additional services — it’s total stewardship from application to graduation.

4. How involved do you work with the family?
Answer:
We provide advisory access and strategic updates to families throughout the cycle.
For Executive Advisory clients, we offer full consultation on decision-making, timing, financial aid, and strategic positioning, ensuring parents and families are aligned and informed at every stage.

5. How does your pay-upon-admission model work?
Answer:
Our model aligns with the serious, investment-focused approach of our clients:
Engagement begins with strategy and mentorship
Payment is made only upon official admission to an Ivy League university
This ensures that our incentives are aligned with your child’s success, while protecting families from upfront risk
Disclaimer: Admission decisions remain solely with the universities

6. How do you maintain ethics and authenticity?
Answer:
We follow strict international academic integrity standards:
Candidates remain the original author of all materials
Our team guides, structures, and refines based on interviews and insights from the candidate
Essays, CVs, SOPs, and scholarship applications always reflect the applicant’s voice, experience, and intellectual ownership
This is fully transparent and Ivy League–safe.



7. Do you offer visa assistance?
Answer:
Yes. For Executive Advisory clients, we provide:
F-1 / J-1 visa strategy
Review of I-20 / DS-2019 and financial documentation
Mock visa interview preparation and risk mitigation
Guidance on timing, submission, and integration into the U.S. academic system

8. Do you assist with financial aid and scholarships?
Answer:
Absolutely. We guide candidates through every stage of financial aid and scholarship applications:
Identifying funding opportunities and eligibility
Preparing supporting documents and forms
Submission tracking and deadline management
Alignment with academic narrative and research goals
Our goal is to maximize funding potential while ensuring ethical compliance.

9. Do you support students after admission?
Answer:
Yes. We provide full-cycle stewardship, including:
Enrollment guidance (course registration, housing, orientation)
Retention support (milestone check-ins, scholarship renewals, academic progress)
Graduation readiness and completion support
Career transition and further academic advising if desired
We ensure the student’s journey is managed meticulously from application to graduation, giving families peace of mind.



10. When should a family start the process?
Answer:
The earlier, the better. Ivy League admissions are strategic and cumulative.
Early engagement allows us to:
Develop academic and research identity
Build leadership and intellectual profile
Align essays, CV, recommendations, and financial aid with the strongest narrative
Late-stage preparation is less effective for high-stakes candidates.

11. Can you handle multiple children or applicants at once?
Answer:
Yes, but capacity is limited to preserve quality and exclusivity.
Each child receives founder-led mentorship and dedicated support.
We prioritize depth over volume to ensure every applicant is positioned at the highest level.

12. What makes your mentorship different from other agencies?
Answer:
Alumni-led (Harvard, Johns Hopkins, KAUST, Harvard Medical School)
Full-cycle, end-to-end support, including financial aid, visa, enrollment, retention, and graduation
Investment-focused, not transactional
Ethical, selective, and high-touch
Focused on developing the candidate, not just submitting applications
We don’t offer shortcuts. We engineer readiness, narrative, and alignment, which is why our clients consistently succeed.
